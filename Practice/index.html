THALAPATHY VIJAY

Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), in February 2024. After announcing his departure from cinema, he has focused on building his political presence. The TVK has been registered with the Election Commission of India and aims to contest the 2026 Tamil Nadu Assembly elections. In July 2025, the TVK declared Vijay its official Chief Ministerial candidate. The party has stated it will not form alliances with the DMK or BJP. Vijay has been actively engaging supporters and building his political platform ahead of the election.
